I'm a Ph.D. student in the [Network Security Group (NetSec)](https://netsec.ethz.ch) at [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch), advised by Professor [Adrian Perrig](https://netsec.ethz.ch/people/aperrig/).

Before joining NetSec, I received my master degree from [National Taiwan University](https://www.ntu.edu.tw/english) advised by Professor [Hsu-Chun Hsiao](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/~hchsiao/). 
During the study I worked on a group key agreement protocol to protect user privacy against malicious chatbots.

I'm interested in security protocol designs, protocol verification, anonymous communication, and quantum resilience.
